School Hours

Classes meet Monday through Friday. Our school day is offered in three formats: morning, afternoon, and full day. Families can use the age groups below to quickly find the program hours that match their child.

Daily Schedule Formats

  • Afternoon program: 12:30 p.m. - 3:15 p.m.
  • Full day program: 9:00 a.m. - 3:15 p.m.
